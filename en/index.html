Frequently asked questions

Before you get in touch

What does a communication for development (C4D) consultant do?

A C4D expert designs communication strategies and campaigns that serve development goals: behaviour change, social mobilisation, advocacy and visibility of donor-funded programmes. I cover the whole chain, from strategy to content production.

What types of organisations do you work with?

Bilateral and multilateral development agencies (USAID, UNDP, UNESCO), international NGOs (OXFAM), foundations, the British Council, large companies committed to CSR, as well as incubators and entrepreneurship support programmes.

Do you work outside Senegal?

Yes. I am based in Dakar and work across West Africa and internationally, on site or remotely, in English and French.

Do you respond to calls for proposals and tenders?

Yes. I have solid experience of international donor procurement procedures and produce technical and financial proposals in English and French.

What does creative industries entrepreneurship consulting cover?

Supporting entrepreneurs and programmes in the cultural and creative industries: music, audiovisual, fashion, design, tourism, sport, media. Business model structuring, investment readiness, founder coaching and delivery of acceleration programmes.
