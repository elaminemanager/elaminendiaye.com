Frequently asked questions

Your questions about a C4D assignment

Can you manage a full mandate, from strategy to production?

Yes. That is precisely the value of my profile: a single point of contact for strategy, creative work and production, with a multidisciplinary team available in Dakar.

Are you familiar with international donor procedures?

Yes. I have responded to many USAID, United Nations, European Union and bilateral donor consultations and know the related visibility and accountability requirements.

In which languages do you produce?

English, French and Wolof, with a network of translators and voice talents for other West African national languages.

What are your lead times?

A strategy is scoped in two to four weeks; a full campaign with production takes six to twelve weeks depending on scale. I propose a schedule from the first discussion.

A programme to make visible, a behaviour to shift?

Describe your context and timeline. I will come back with a first proposed approach.
